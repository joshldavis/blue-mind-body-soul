The fight is doing a job

Hey friend,

If you have more than one kid, you know the afternoon soundtrack: "She's looking at me." "That's not fair!" Then the slammed door — and you, walking in tired, trying to figure out who started it.

Here's what behavioral psychology suggests instead: "who started it" is almost never the useful question. Sibling fights follow loops — a trigger, a behavior, and a payoff. And very often, the payoff is us. Our full, undivided attention.

The key takeaway: Treat the system, not only the behavior. Track fights for 48 hours, figure out what each fight is getting each child, set three simple house rules, ignore low-level bickering, calmly remove for aggression, and praise cooperation loudly. Give it two weeks — and expect it to get a little worse before it gets better.
