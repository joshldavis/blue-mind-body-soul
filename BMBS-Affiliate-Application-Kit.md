## When links arrive: how to swap them in

Each product currently appears as plain bold text (e.g. `<strong>AG1</strong>`). To monetize, wrap it:

```html
<strong><a href="YOUR_TRACKING_LINK" rel="sponsored nofollow" target="_blank">AG1</a></strong>
```

Use `rel="sponsored nofollow"` (Google + FTC best practice). Tell me when you have links and I can do the swaps across the blog, social, and newsletter files for you.
