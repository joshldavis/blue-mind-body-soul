10-Slide Carousel Script

Slide 1 (Hook): Slept terribly last night? The two things your body is begging for — sleeping in + more coffee — are exactly what turn ONE bad night into a bad week. Save this. 🧵
Slide 2: The core rule: you can't "make up" lost sleep by chasing it. The best recovery is protecting TONIGHT. Do that and you reset in 24 hours.
Slide 3: ❌ Mistake #1: Sleeping in. Going more than ~1 hr past your normal wake time bleeds off the sleep pressure (adenosine) you need tonight AND pushes your clock later. Tomorrow gets worse.
Slide 4: ✅ Fix: Get up at your normal time. Your wake time is your strongest body-clock anchor. Protect it even when you're exhausted.
Slide 5: ✅ Sunlight in your eyes within 30–60 min of waking. 5–10 min outside (even cloudy) triggers a healthy morning cortisol spike — the hormone that wakes you up AND sets tonight's melatonin timer.
Slide 6: ✅ Delay caffeine 90–120 min. Slam coffee at wake-up and the mid-morning crash hits twice as hard on no sleep. Hydrate first. Coffee lands better on a cleaner slate.
Slide 7: ✅ Move in the morning. Even a 20-min walk reinforces the cortisol peak and nudges your clock earlier — a real, non-chemical alertness boost.
Slide 8: ❌ Mistake #2: The afternoon coffee. Caffeine's half-life is 5–8 hrs. A 3pm cup is still half-active at 9pm — sabotaging the exact night you need to go well.
Slide 9: ✅ Recharge instead with a 20-MIN-MAX nap (longer = groggy "sleep inertia") OR 10–30 min of NSDR (non-sleep deep rest). Rest adds energy; coffee borrows it from tonight.
Slide 10 (CTA): One bad night isn't a crisis — the panicked overcorrection is. Run the protocol, trust the reset. Full guide at bluemindbodyandsoul.com (link in bio). 💙
