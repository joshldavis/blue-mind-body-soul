Caption

1 in 5 brains works differently. And most of them spent their childhood being told they were behind. The science has finally caught up with what dyslexic adults have known forever: dyslexia is not a deficit. It is a different cognitive style. The same neural wiring that makes decoding letters harder makes pattern recognition, imagination, big-picture reasoning, and empathy easier. 84% of dyslexics score above average at imagining. 84% at reasoning. 80% at connecting with other people. These are not soft consolation prizes for the spelling. They are the actual cognitive profile. And here is the part the future of work is finally noticing: the skills AI cannot replicate — lateral thinking, originality, reading a room, seeing the pattern no one else sees — are exactly the skills the dyslexic brain is wired for. The cost of NOT seeing this is real. 66% of HR leaders think their workplaces support dyslexic thinkers. Only 14% of actual dyslexics agree. That gap is a mental health risk. It is also a missed opportunity at the org level. If you are dyslexic, or you love someone who is, please hear this: you are not broken. You are wired for a different draft of intelligence — and one that is finally being named. Save this post. Send it to a parent, a teacher, or a friend who needed to read it years ago.
