Slide 7: Two laws to tape to your monitor. PARETO: ~80% of results come from ~20% of effort. PARKINSON: work expands to fill the time you give it. Do less of what doesn't matter. Shrink the container.
Slide 8: Go on an INFORMATION DIET. Batch email to 1–2 times a day. Protect unbroken attention. Every interruption has a switching cost — and the fragmented day is both less productive AND more stressful.
Slide 9: MINI-RETIREMENTS > someday. Distribute rest through your life instead of hoarding it for the end. Recovery isn't a reward — it's physiology. Small, regular doses beat one heroic escape.

You just have to design a little more time back into your life: find your most valuable 20%, shrink one task (work expands to fill the time you give it), batch your email, and put one restorative block on the calendar before the year fills up around it.
