Caption

That 9pm reach for something sweet? Not a character flaw.

For two million years, sweetness meant rare, safe, precious calories — ripe fruit and wild honey. Evolution built us a powerful craving for it and never bothered with an off switch, because it didn't need one. The scarcity was the off switch.

Then we learned to make sugar in unlimited quantity, cheap, and engineered into nearly everything we eat. The accelerator is ancient. The brake belonged to the world — and we removed it.

So the struggle with sugar isn't about willpower. It's about MISMATCH: an ancient, intelligent appetite loose in a world that has learned to exploit it with extraordinary precision. You're not weak. You're the Man of Bicorp, equipped for scarcity and living in a flood.

The gentle answer isn't guilt or purging. It's restoring rarity — letting the sweet thing be a real treat again, rare enough to delight and good enough to deserve full attention. The pleasure is greater, not lesser, for it.
